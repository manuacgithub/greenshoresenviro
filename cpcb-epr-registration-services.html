What is EPR
In the context of the CPCB (Central Pollution Control Board) in India, EPR stands for Extended Producer Responsibility.

In India, Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) Certification is a mandatory CPCB compliance for all Producers, Importers, and Brand Owners (PIBOs). This framework legally requires businesses to manage and recycle the post-consumer waste generated by their products—including plastic packaging, e-waste, batteries, and tyres

Core Waste Categories Regulated by CPCB

Educate your visitors on the specific regulatory frameworks managed under the centralized portal.

📦

Plastic Waste Management (PWM)

Regulated under the Plastic Waste Management Rules, targeting all packaging sub-categories:

  • Category I: Rigid plastic packaging.
  • Category II: Flexible plastic packaging (single or multi-layer).
  • Category III: Multi-layered plastic packaging (at least one layer of plastic and other materials).
  • Category IV: Compostable plastic sheets and packaging.
🔌

E-Waste (Management) Rules

Mandatory recycling targets for producers and importers of Electrical and Electronic Equipment (EEE), covering information technology, telecommunications, and consumer appliances.

🔋

Battery Waste Management

Applicable to all types of batteries (EV, industrial, automotive, and portable), ensuring safe collection, refurbishment, or material recovery targets are logged on the portal.

🛞

Waste Tyre Management

Regulated targets ensuring that domestic manufacturing and tyre imports are offset by verified recycling and rubber/pyrolysis processing credits.
