FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about KSPCB consent, compliance, and environmental regulations in Karnataka.

KSPCB (Karnataka State Pollution Control Board) consent is a mandatory approval for any industry or establishment that generates air emissions, liquid effluents, or solid/hazardous waste. Operating without valid consent is a legal violation under the Water Act (1974) and Air Act (1981), and can result in closure notices, penalties, or criminal liability.

CFE (Consent for Establishment) — obtained before construction begins. It is a No Objection Certificate confirming the proposed activity meets environmental norms.

CFO (Consent for Operation) — obtained after construction is complete and before production begins. Both are mandatory under KSPCB regulations.

Timelines depend on the industry category (Red, Orange, Green, or White):
— Green / White: 15–30 days
— Orange: 30–60 days
— Red: 60–90 days (may involve site inspection)
Delays most often occur due to incomplete applications. Green Shores ensures all submissions are complete on the first attempt.

Core documents include: site plan and layout, process flow diagram, raw material and product details, ETP/STP design, pollution control measures, land ownership/lease documents, local body NOC, and factory licence. Additional documents may be required based on industry category. Green Shores prepares and verifies all documentation before submission.

Yes, if your industry generates process effluent or sewage that cannot meet KSPCB discharge norms without treatment. An ETP is required for industrial process wastewater. An STP is mandatory for establishments generating 20+ KLD of sewage. KSPCB will not grant CFO without a functional ETP/STP where applicable.

ZLD is a treatment approach where all process effluent is recycled within the facility — zero liquid waste discharged to the environment. KSPCB and the NGT mandate ZLD for industries such as textile dyeing, tanneries, distilleries, sugar mills, and certain pharmaceutical units. Green Shores designs cost-effective ZLD systems tailored to your industry scale.

Monthly returns include effluent quality data, stack emission reports, and water consumption details. Annual filings cover a comprehensive environmental performance report. Failure to submit on time can result in consent suspension. Green Shores manages all recurring filings so nothing lapses.

Under the Hazardous and Other Wastes Rules (2016), industries must obtain KSPCB authorisation, maintain a waste manifest system, store waste in designated areas, and dispose only through KSPCB-approved recyclers or TSDFs. Green Shores handles authorisation, waste audits, and coordination with approved disposal agencies.

Under the E-Waste (Management) Rules 2022, producers, manufacturers, bulk consumers, and dealers of electrical/electronic equipment must obtain EPR (Extended Producer Responsibility) authorisation from CPCB. This includes IT companies, hospitals, educational institutions, and manufacturing firms. Green Shores manages the entire EPR registration and annual compliance process.

Operating with lapsed consent is treated as operating without consent — consequences include show-cause notices, closure directions, financial penalties, and criminal prosecution of company officers. Renewal applications must be submitted at least 4 months before expiry. Green Shores tracks renewal calendars for all clients and initiates the process proactively to ensure zero lapses.
